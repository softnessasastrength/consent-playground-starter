# Experience Design

## Core statement

Consent is not a checkbox. It is a living state shaped by specificity, context, freedom, and time.

## Three-minute arc

1. **Invitation** — Introduce consent as a conversation.
2. **Boundary** — Show that a yes may be conditional.
3. **Uncertainty** — Demonstrate that hesitation requires a pause.
4. **Withdrawal** — Make clear that consent can end immediately.
5. **Reflection** — Reveal the state transitions and principles.

## Design principles

- Calm rather than alarmist
- Clear without becoming clinical
- Inclusive and non-gendered
- Private and fully offline
- Accessible without relying on color alone
- Focused on respectful decision-making, not punishment

## Non-goals

- Recreating the entire Softness as Strength app
- Collecting real intimate information
- Providing legal determinations
- Simulating graphic sexual content
